    🔒 100% On-Device & Private. Absolutely everything runs locally in your browser. Video frames are processed live in volatile memory using local canvas pixel-differencing and are immediately discarded — no video, frames, audio, or telemetry are ever recorded, transmitted, or stored on any server. There are no tracking scripts, analytics, or external servers. All workout stats, streaks, and history live exclusively in your device's browser localStorage.
    


    How the counting works: The camera analyzes rhythmic motion energy across frames with exercise-specific two-stroke cycles (e.g. descent + ascent for squats). Slide Sensitivity to calibrate to your room's lighting and distance. Framing: standing work full-body and face-on; pushups, mountain climbers, and lunges read cleanest side-on with the phone placed low.
