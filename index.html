    How the counting works. Frames from your camera are compared to each other inside this page —
    a moving-average motion signal, band-passed, with one rep counted per motion cycle and a minimum
    gap per exercise (0.26 s for hops, 0.85 s for squats and pushups). No video, no frames and no model
    calls ever leave the device; there is no server in this page. It reads rhythm, not joint angles, so
    a shaky count is normal on the first try — slide Sensitivity until the ticks on the trace line up
    with your reps, or use Tap to count. Framing: standing work full-body and face-on; pushups,
    mountain climbers and lunges read best side-on with the phone low.
